# Development Economics (0388 21 DS 2201) — Academic Learning Portal

An all-in-one, interactive web platform built specifically for the **Second Year Second Term** course: **Development Economics** (`Course Code: 0388 21 DS 2201`, 3.0 Credits, Core Course).

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 📖 Full Textbook-Grade Curriculum (8 Chapters)
* **Section A (Chapters 1 to 4):**
  1. *Introducing Development Economics:* Growth vs. Development, Goulet's Core Values, Sen's Capabilities, Growth Components.
  2. *Measuring Development:* GDP, GNP/GNI, NFIA, Nominal vs. Real, Per Capita Income pitfalls, Purchasing Power Parity (PPP), Balassa-Samuelson effect.
  3. *Growth Theories & Models:* Adam Smith, Ricardo, Marx, Schumpeter, Keynes, Rostow's Stages, Balanced vs. Unbalanced (Hirschman), Big Push (Rosenstein-Rodan), Harrod-Domar ($g = s / c$), Solow-Swan Neoclassical model, Endogenous Growth (Romer, Lucas).
  4. *Human Development:* Mahbub ul Haq & Amartya Sen paradigm, Human Capital vs. Human Development, HDI Geometric Mean formula, IHDI, GDI, GII, and MPI.
* **Section B (Chapters 5 to 8):**
  5. *Industrialization:* Lewis Dual-Sector model, Import-Substitution (ISI) vs. Export-Promotion (EPI), East Asian Tigers experience, External Debt & Debt Overhang, Foreign Direct Investment (FDI), Local Economic Development (LED) clusters.
  6. *Poverty:* Absolute vs. Relative, BBS Cost of Basic Needs (CBN) method (2,122 kcal + non-food allowances), Headcount Index ($H$), Foster-Greer-Thorbecke (FGT) class of poverty measures ($P_0, P_1, P_2$), Multidimensional Poverty Index (MPI).
  7. *Inequality:* Functional vs. Personal distribution, Lorenz Curve, Gini Coefficient ($G = A / (A+B)$), Kuznets Inverted-U Hypothesis, Piketty's critique ($r > g$).
  8. *Institutions & Development:* Douglass North's formal/informal rules, Permanent Settlement of 1793 & Bengal land tenure, Sharecropping (*borga*) & Marshallian inefficiency, Transaction Costs (Ronald Coase), Property Rights (Hernando de Soto), Inclusive vs. Extractive Institutions (Acemoglu & Robinson - *Why Nations Fail*), Democracy and development.

### 2. 📝 Exam Question Bank with High-Scoring Benchmark Answers
Directly incorporates the exact questions from your class test photos, structured by difficulty:
* **Easy (2 Marks):** 1 A4 page equivalent. Tightly focused definitions, mechanisms, and formulas.
* **Medium (5 Marks):** 3-4 A4 pages equivalent. Substantive comparative matrices, diagrams, policy trade-offs (e.g., RMG sector growth vs development, Public resources trade-off between infrastructure vs education vs R&D, District A vs District B HDI analysis).
* **Hard (10 Marks):** Multi-part exam questions with step-by-step mathematical calculations:
  * **Question 1 (Poverty):** Khulna 5-household survey (Tk 4000, 5000, 5800, 7500, 10000; poverty line Tk 6000) with complete $P_0, P_1, P_2$ derivations and Sen's monotonicity/transfer axioms.
  * **Question 2 (Inequality):** Average income vs distribution, Richest 20% vs Poorest 20% quintile share ratio, Lorenz curve, and Gini coefficient.
  * **Question 3 (Institutions):** Rural Khulna agricultural traders cartel, transparent pricing, independent weighing, farmer cooperatives, and elite capture (*dadankari*) solutions.

### 4. 📊 Economics Calculators & Empirical Lab
* **FGT Poverty Calculator:** Preloaded with the Khulna exam question dataset + custom inputs. Calculates $P_0$ (Headcount), $P_1$ (Poverty Gap), $P_2$ (Severity), and exact monthly transfer budgets needed to eradicate poverty.
* **Lorenz Curve & Gini Simulator:** Live interactive SVG graph plotting the 45-degree line of perfect equality and the Lorenz curve, calculating Area A, Area B, Gini index, and Kuznets quintile ratio.
* **Harrod-Domar & Financing Gap Calculator:** Calculates attainable growth rate and foreign aid financing gap based on national savings rate and ICOR.
* **HDI Calculator:** Computes Health, Education, and Income sub-indices and overall Geometric Mean HDI.
